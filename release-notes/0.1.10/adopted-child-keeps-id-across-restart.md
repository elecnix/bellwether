An adopted child whose harness quits and resumes the same session in its pane stays the same child, under the same id: the watcher claims the pane again on its machine, and its session gets `parent_report` and its messages back. Adopting such a pane again keeps the id too, and when the far machine holds the session under another id, the reply gives the id it replaced.
