`agent_teleport` and `bellwether teleport` move a live or stopped child to another machine under the same child id. Before its first turn, its session receives a message that gives both machines, both directories, who asked and why. Bellwether never types it into the pane. Bellwether refuses a missing destination directory, an older destination harness, a destination with no inbound path and an adopted child. Worktrees are unchanged: the requester prepares the destination.
