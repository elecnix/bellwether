When a daemon restart interrupts a move or a teleport, the next daemon finishes or undoes it, going by what both machines show. A destination that runs the session keeps it. Otherwise the copy goes, and a child that ran resumes on its source. While a child crosses, `agent_list` and `bellwether doctor` report it as moving, not exited. A child that a crossing leaves on neither machine gets a doctor `fail` line with the resume that brings it back.
