A child's record now keeps every place the child left by a move or a teleport under `departures`, where it kept only the last one. So a chain of moves keeps the worktree on the parent's machine within reach: retirement removes each worktree left there, and gives each one on another machine with its machine. The parent's daemon also drains each of those machines once more. A record from an older Bellwether, with only `movedFrom`, reads as one entry.
