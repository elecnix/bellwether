A child that comes back while the daemon retires it keeps its worktree. The cleanup takes the same child lock as a resume, and it reads the record again before it removes anything. Workspaces close safely too. A workspace Bellwether made for a child no longer closes after a returning child opens a tab in it, since the tab list and the close now share one turn with that tab, and a child that comes back after the close opens in a new workspace under the same label.
