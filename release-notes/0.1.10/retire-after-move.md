A child on another machine can now call `request_retirement`. The daemon there replies with the parent's id and passes the request to that parent's daemon through its relay outbox, as it passes a report. For a child that moved from the parent's machine, that daemon retires the child's left worktree once herdr drops the child's pane. Until the parent's daemon collects the request, `bellwether doctor` on the child's machine prints a line for it.
