A child Bellwether started can teleport itself with `agent_teleport` and no `child`. The reply comes first. The daemon waits for the turn to end, within 5 minutes, and teleports the child as its parent. Its notice says the child asked. A stall or a refusal leaves `teleportStalled` and a doctor line. The notice no longer quotes a session id: it reads "at your parent's request" or "at your own request", and the record keeps the parent id or `self`.
