Bellwether now refuses an act on a child when two sources of authority disagree about who holds it. The refusal lists both sources, and Bellwether doesn't pick one. Before this change, a parent whose adopted child's machine had given the pane to another parent kept typing its messages into that pane. When two parents' registries listed the same pane, or a registry and a far parent's claim covered the same session, the lookup's first match decided the holder. `agent_move` now refuses an adopted child, as `agent_restart` and `agent_teleport` already did, because a move closes the pane it leaves and Bellwether closes only panes it started. `agent_send` and a resume refuse a child whose adoption its machine has not confirmed, and a child whose session now stands under another child id. `bellwether doctor` prints one `authority:` line per live child, naming who holds it, on which machine and by what evidence, and a `fail` line for each pane whose holders disagree. [Authority](https://elecnix.github.io/bellwether/authority.html) gives the decision table.
