When `relay-cleanup` fails at a child's exit on another machine, as when the relay is down, the parent's watcher retries it, waiting longer after each failure. It retries for 5 minutes after the first failure, and whenever the relay to that machine comes back, even after that. The child remains exited throughout. After 5 minutes, `bellwether doctor` prints a `warn` line that lists what remains on that machine and the command that removes it by hand.
