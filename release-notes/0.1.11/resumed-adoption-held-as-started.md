A resume now brings an adopted child back as one Bellwether started. A `/clear` in its new pane keeps the same child under the new session, and its adapter remains connected to the pane. Retirement takes its proofs and may remove its worktree, and `agent_restart` and `agent_teleport` accept it. The record keeps `origin: adopt` as provenance. When the session returns under an adoption, by hand or through `agent_adopt`, the child counts as adopted again.
