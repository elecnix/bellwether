A session's restart of itself waits at most 5 minutes for its turn to end. The daemon keeps the session's own events unread while it waits, so they don't start another turn, and the new session receives them. A second call and doctor report the pane, the status herdr gives for it and the events held. A wait that runs out closes nothing and records the cause as `restartStalled`.
